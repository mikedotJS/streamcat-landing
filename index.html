Three steps, and your console is shared

  1. Plug in and open

    Plug your capture card in over USB, start your console, then open StreamCat. The picture shows up on its own.

  2. Share the window

    In your screen-sharing tool (Discord, for example), pick the “StreamCat” window from the list.

  3. Keep the sound on

    Make sure sound sharing is on before you start. Others see and hear your game.
